✅ **Why use a logger in Node.js?**

1. 📄 **Track Requests & Responses:**  
   - Logs incoming requests, status codes, and response times.  

2. 🐞 **Debugging & Troubleshooting:**  
   - Helps identify issues by reviewing logs.  

3. 📊 **Monitor Application Activity:**  
   - Tracks user interactions and errors.  

4. 🔥 **Error Tracking:**  
   - Logs errors for later analysis and debugging.  

5. 📚 **Audit & Compliance:**  
   - Keeps records of operations for security and auditing.  

✔️ **Tools:** `morgan`, `winston`, `pino` are popular Node.js loggers.
